# EX.NO.3 – SCENARIO-BASED REPORT DEVELOPMENT USING DIFFERENT PROMPTING TECHNIQUES

### Name: PRADEEBA V
### Register Number: 212225040306

# AIM

To design and evaluate different prompt engineering techniques, namely Straightforward Prompting, Tabular Format Prompting, Preceding Question Prompting and Missing Word Prompting, using an AI-based Smart Education Assistant scenario.

# USE CASE: AI-BASED SMART EDUCATION ASSISTANT

## Scenario

An educational institution plans to introduce an AI-powered Smart Education Assistant to improve the learning experience of students. The system helps students understand difficult subjects, access study materials, receive assignment notifications, prepare for examinations and clarify academic doubts.

The assistant provides personalized learning suggestions based on students' interests and learning progress. It also helps teachers share learning resources, manage academic activities and provide additional support to students.

The main objective is to make education more accessible, improve student engagement and simplify academic management through Artificial Intelligence.

---

# 1. STRAIGHTFORWARD PROMPTING

## Description

Straightforward prompting involves giving the AI a direct and simple instruction without providing extensive background information. It is useful for obtaining quick and relevant answers.

## Prompt

Design an AI-based Smart Education Assistant that helps students understand academic subjects, access study materials, receive assignment notifications and prepare for examinations.

Explain its major features and how they support students in their learning process.

## Generated Response

The AI-based Smart Education Assistant is designed to make learning easier and more effective for students. It helps students clarify academic doubts, understand difficult concepts and access study materials whenever required. The system provides assignment reminders, examination preparation tips and personalized learning suggestions based on individual progress. It can also recommend additional resources such as videos, notes and practice questions. Teachers can use the assistant to share educational materials and communicate important academic information. Overall, the system improves learning efficiency, saves time and provides students with continuous academic support.

## Evaluation

- The prompt gives a clear description of the expected system.
- Produces a relevant response without complicated instructions.
- Covers the major features of the education assistant.
- Easy to understand and suitable for beginners.
- Useful for generating general descriptions quickly.

---

# 2. TABULAR FORMAT PROMPTING

## Description

Tabular format prompting instructs the AI to present information in rows and columns. This approach makes information more organized and helps users understand different features through direct comparison.

## Prompt

Present the important features of an AI-based Smart Education Assistant in a table with the following columns:

1. Feature
2. Functionality
3. Example

Include at least five features that help students and teachers in academic activities.

## Generated Response

| Feature | Functionality | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Doubt Clarification | Answers students' academic questions. | Explains a difficult mathematics problem. |
| Study Material Access | Provides relevant educational resources. | Shares notes and reference materials for Java. |
| Assignment Notifications | Reminds students about submission deadlines. | Sends an alert before an assignment due date. |
| Personalized Learning | Suggests learning resources based on student progress. | Recommends additional programming exercises. |
| Exam Preparation | Helps students prepare through practice activities. | Generates mock questions for an examination. |
| Progress Tracking | Monitors learning activities and performance. | Displays completed lessons and quiz scores. |

## Evaluation

- Presents information in a systematic format.
- Makes different functionalities easier to compare.
- Improves readability and organization.
- Clearly identifies the purpose of each feature.
- Particularly useful for reports and academic documentation.

---

# 3. PRECEDING QUESTION PROMPTING

## Description

Preceding Question Prompting involves asking a question that establishes the context of a topic before requesting a detailed response. It encourages the AI to provide an explanation based on the background and purpose of the question.

## Prompt

Why is Artificial Intelligence becoming important in modern education? How can an AI-based Smart Education Assistant help students overcome learning difficulties, manage assignments, access educational resources and improve their academic performance?

Based on these points, explain the role and importance of a Smart Education Assistant.

## Generated Response

Artificial Intelligence is becoming increasingly important in education because it provides personalized learning experiences and makes academic support more accessible. Students often face difficulties understanding complex subjects, managing assignments and preparing for examinations. An AI-based Smart Education Assistant helps address these challenges by providing instant explanations, recommending suitable study materials and sending reminders about important academic activities.

It can identify areas where students need improvement and suggest additional exercises to strengthen their understanding. Students can also use it to practice questions and revise important topics. Teachers benefit from easier communication and better organization of learning resources.

Thus, an AI-based education assistant supports both students and teachers by improving learning efficiency, encouraging independent study and making academic activities easier to manage.

## Evaluation

- Provides a detailed explanation of the given topic.
- Establishes a clear connection between the question and the response.
- Demonstrates contextual understanding.
- Explains the importance of AI in education.
- Suitable for descriptive and analytical questions.

---

# 4. MISSING WORD PROMPTING

## Description

Missing Word Prompting presents incomplete sentences and asks the AI to fill in the missing words based on the given context. It helps evaluate the AI's ability to understand sentence meaning and identify appropriate words.

## Prompt

Complete the following sentences using suitable words related to the AI-based Smart Education Assistant.

1. Students can clarify their academic __________ using the AI assistant.
2. The system provides access to digital study __________.
3. Students receive __________ about assignment deadlines.
4. Personalized learning suggestions are based on student __________.
5. The assistant helps students prepare for their __________.
6. Teachers can share educational __________ through the platform.

## Generated Response

1. Doubts
2. Materials
3. Notifications
4. Progress
5. Examinations
6. Resources

## Evaluation

- Tests the AI's ability to understand context.
- Identifies appropriate words to complete sentences.
- Reinforces important concepts related to AI in education.
- Produces short and understandable answers.
- Useful for quizzes, assessments and learning activities.

# COMPARISON OF PROMPT ENGINEERING TECHNIQUES

The following table compares the four prompting techniques based on their purpose, advantages and suitable applications.

| Prompting Technique | Main Purpose | Key Advantage | Suitable Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| Straightforward Prompting | Provides direct instructions to the AI. | Simple and quick responses. | General explanations and descriptions. |
| Tabular Format Prompting | Organizes information into tables. | Better readability and comparison. | Reports and structured documentation. |
| Preceding Question Prompting | Establishes context through questions. | Generates detailed and relevant explanations. | Analytical and descriptive tasks. |
| Missing Word Prompting | Completes incomplete sentences. | Tests contextual understanding. | Quizzes and educational assessments. |

# CONCLUSION

Prompt Engineering is an important technique for improving communication with Artificial Intelligence systems. Through this experiment, four different prompting methods were explored using the AI-based Smart Education Assistant scenario.

Straightforward Prompting helped generate direct responses, Tabular Format Prompting organized information clearly, Preceding Question Prompting produced contextual explanations, and Missing Word Prompting tested the ability to identify suitable words based on sentence meaning.

The experiment shows that providing appropriate instructions and selecting the correct prompting technique can improve the relevance, clarity and organization of AI-generated responses.

# RESULT

Thus, four different prompt engineering techniques, namely Straightforward Prompting, Tabular Format Prompting, Preceding Question Prompting and Missing Word Prompting, were successfully designed and evaluated using the AI-based Smart Education Assistant scenario. Their different approaches and applications were studied and compared.
